# The Linen name and logo

Linen's **code** is free software under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). The **name "Linen"** (as the
name of this video app) and the **thread logo** (a play button drawn as one thread, in
`packaging/linen.svg`, `assets/logo-mark.svg` and `docs/`) are not part of that license. They
belong to Bugzy and tell people they're getting the official app.

**You can, without asking:**
- say your app is "based on Linen" or "a fork of Linen", and link here;
- use the name and logo to talk about Linen itself, in articles, videos, reviews and
  tutorials;
- package the official, unchanged Linen for a Linux distribution or app store under its own
  name and logo, as long as it's clear who made it.

**You can't, without written permission:**
- publish or sell a changed version under the name Linen, or with the Linen logo;
- use the name or logo in a way that makes people think your app, product or service is the
  official Linen or is endorsed by its author.

Forks are welcome: give yours its own name and logo.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue at https://github.com/BugzySleeps/Linen/issues.
